ABOUT HYPERCUBE

A small, incisive and expert consultancy with a passion for sports and mobility. That is Hypercube in a nutshell. Our team consists of passionate people who do what they do best and believe in what they do. In the more than 20 years that we have existed, we have built up extensive experience in the world of sports and public transport that we dare to say is unique. Not only in the Netherlands but worldwide.

We support our clients with economic challenges and innovations. We combine our knowledge of data with a people-oriented approach in which we enrich and deepen data with the input of important stakeholders. We use proven effective models and formulas and stand for a clear and understandable process approach.

We proudly go by the name Hypercube. Why? A Hypercube is a cube with multiple dimensions. For us, this symbolizes looking at a challenge from multiple perspectives. We make connections with an open mind in order to arrive at a well-founded and supported result and, last but not least: a pleasant collaboration!

Want to know more? Please feel free to contact us

MEET OUR TEAM

Pieter Nieuwenhuis

Founder/CEO

Pieter is director and founder of Hypercube (in 2000). From the beginning, he focused on value-chain-transcending economic analysis. His objective, down-to-earth view and powers of persuasion help decision-makers to make decisions. This has enabled Hypercube to grow into a unique and fully-fledged player in the international sports world and national public transport. Projects that characterise Hypercube's work include the SCBA for the public transport chip card he developed in 2003 for the Ministry of Transport, Public Works and Water Management. In 2007, he was at the drawing board of UEFA Champions League and Europa League. Pieter studied Mathematics and Computer Science at Utrecht University and subsequently worked for Capgemini, Computer Sciences Corporation and Aurora Business Consulting.
Pim Klaver

Director of Sports

Pim has been working at Hypercube as a project manager since 2018. After an international cycling career, he studied Econometrics. Thanks to his econometrics and journalism background, he is able to come up with concrete solutions to issues. Pim is very good at making complex issues understandable and is able to keep an overview of different interests and perspectives. He is mainly active in international football (UEFA and CONCACAF).
John Kleppe

COO/CINO

John is a consultant and, as Innovation team leader, responsible for Hypercube's solid models and analyses, as well as their scientific substantiation. He has been working for Hypercube since 2014 and is a key contributor to many projects in the fields of sports and mobility, particularly in the areas of rankings and competition models (sports), and scenario studies and revenue forecasting (mobility). With a PhD in applied mathematics (game theory), he is a strong analytical thinker, in whom creativity and structure go hand in hand in the search for solutions. John also understands how to present them clearly.
Jan Willem Mulder

Lead Consultant Mobility

Jan Willem has been working as a consultant at Hypercube since 2018. He has extensive experience with mobility issues in the field of data-driven research, monitor assignments, design and revenue distribution of interoperable subscriptions and impact analyses. In addition, he has built up extensive domain knowledge around relevant topics in public transport. At Hypercube, his responsibilities include the set-up and maintenance of various BI portals. He is also involved in developments around the new public transport payment system and the possibilities this offers for products and fares. Jan Willem studied Economics at Utrecht University with a master's degree in International Development.
